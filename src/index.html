Story

The Shattered Heartstone

Cubara is a floating island, kept alive by the glowing Heartstone. One night the Golden Overlord, who wants to be the buffest thing in the sky, smashed it. He ate one shard and threw the other nine to his gym buddies.

Every shard turns whoever holds it into a huge, muscly boss, and its glow is waking up the mobs. You are the last Blockfire: a builder who can shape blocks and fire Ember. With Grandma Brick and her lantern-bot Pip, go and get the shards back.
